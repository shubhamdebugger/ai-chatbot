## D05.6 Disclosure – Conflicts of Interest in Recommendations

Questions this answers: disclosure · advisory dete ho kya · conflict of interest

1. The Research Analyst and its officers / employees do not trade in securities that are the subject of recommendations.
2. There are no actual or potential conflicts of interest with any issuer. Any that arise will be disclosed to the client.
3. No compensation has been received from the subject company of a recommendation.
4. The RA has not managed or co-managed any public offering of any company.
5. No compensation has been received for investment banking, merchant banking or brokerage services from the subject company.
6. No compensation has been received for any other products or services from the subject company.
7. No compensation or benefit has been received from the subject company or a third party in connection with the recommendation.
8. The subject company was not a client of the RA in the 12 months before the recommendation.
9. The RA and its people have not served as officer, director or employee of the subject company.
10. The RA has not engaged in market-making activity in the subject company.

# E02 – Investor Charter (Research Analyst)

## E02.1 Investor Charter – Vision and Mission

Questions this answers: investor charter · mere rights kya hai · SEBI mein complaint kaise kare · SEBI ka address

1. Vision: Invest with knowledge and safety.
2. Mission: Every investor should be able to invest in the right products based on their needs, manage and monitor them to meet their goals, access reports and enjoy financial wellness.

## E02.2 Investor Charter – Business Done by the Research Analyst

Questions this answers: investor charter · mere rights kya hai · SEBI mein complaint kaise kare · SEBI ka address

1. Publish research reports based on the RA's research activities.
2. Provide an independent, unbiased view on securities.
3. Offer unbiased recommendations, disclosing financial interests in recommended securities.
4. Give recommendations based on publicly available information and known observations.
5. Conduct an audit every year.
6. Ensure advertisements follow the Advertisement Code for Research Analysts.
7. Keep records of interactions with clients and prospective clients about research services.

## E02.3 Investor Charter – Services Provided to Investors

Questions this answers: investor charter · mere rights kya hai · SEBI mein complaint kaise kare · SEBI ka address

1. Onboarding: sharing terms and conditions and completing KYC of fee-paying clients.
2. Disclosure of material information: business activity, disciplinary history, terms, associates, risks and conflicts of interest.
3. Disclosure of the extent of use of Artificial Intelligence tools in research services.
4. Disclosure of conflicts of interest when distributing third-party research, or a web link to those disclosures.
5. Disclosure of conflicts between research services and other activities of the RA.
6. Distribution of research reports and recommendations to all clients without discrimination.
7. Confidentiality of research reports until they are public.
8. Respect for data privacy; protection of confidential information.
9. Disclosure of service timelines and keeping to them.
10. Clear guidance and caution notices for complex and high-risk products / services.
11. Honest dealing and integrity with all clients.
12. Confidentiality of client information except for legal obligations or with specific client consent.

## E02.4 Investor Charter – Grievance Redressal (SEBI Route)

Questions this answers: investor charter · mere rights kya hai · SEBI mein complaint kaise kare · SEBI ka address

1. The investor may approach the Research Analyst, who shall resolve the grievance immediately, and not later than 21 days from receipt.
2. Complaints may also be filed on SCORES 2.0, SEBI's online grievance system: first review by the designated body (RAASB), second review by SEBI.
3. If still not satisfied, the investor may use the SMARTODR platform for online conciliation or arbitration.
4. Physical complaints to SEBI: Office of Investor Assistance and Education, Securities and Exchange Board of India, SEBI Bhavan, Plot No. C4-A, 'G' Block, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Bandra (E), Mumbai – 400051.

## E02.5 Investor Charter – Rights of Investors

Questions this answers: investor charter · mere rights kya hai · SEBI mein complaint kaise kare · SEBI ka address

1. Right to privacy and confidentiality.
2. Right to transparent practices.
3. Right to fair and equitable treatment.
4. Right to adequate information.
5. Right to initial and continuing disclosure.
6. Right to receive information about statutory and regulatory disclosures.
7. Right to fair and true advertisement.
8. Right to awareness about service parameters and turnaround times.
9. Right to be informed of timelines for each service.
10. Right to be heard and to satisfactory grievance redressal.
11. Right to timely redressal.
12. Right to exit from a product or service as per agreed terms.
13. Right to clear guidance and caution notices for complex and high-risk products and services.
14. Additional rights for vulnerable consumers, including suitable access for differently abled persons.
15. Right to give feedback on products and services used.
16. Right against coercive, unfair and one-sided clauses in financial agreements.

## E02.6 Investor Charter – Investor Do's

Questions this answers: investor charter · mere rights kya hai · SEBI mein complaint kaise kare · SEBI ka address

1. Always deal with a SEBI-registered Research Analyst.
2. Ensure the RA has a valid registration certificate.
3. Check the SEBI registration number.
4. Read the disclosures in research reports before investing.
5. Pay the RA through banking channels only and keep duly signed receipts. CeFCoM of RAASB may be used if the RA has opted for it.
6. Before buying / selling securities or applying in a public offer, check the recommendation from your RA.
7. Ask questions and clear doubts with your RA before acting on a recommendation.
8. Seek clarification and guidance, especially for complex and high-risk products.
9. Know your right to stop the service as per agreed terms.
10. Know your right to give feedback on services.
11. Know that you are not bound by clauses that go against regulations.
12. Inform SEBI about any RA offering assured or guaranteed returns.

## E02.7 Investor Charter – Investor Don'ts

Questions this answers: investor charter · mere rights kya hai · SEBI mein complaint kaise kare · SEBI ka address

1. Do not give funds for investment to the Research Analyst.
2. Do not fall for luring advertisements or market rumours.
3. Do not be attracted by limited-period discounts, incentives, gifts etc. offered by the RA.
4. Do not share login credentials or passwords of trading, Demat or bank accounts with the RA.

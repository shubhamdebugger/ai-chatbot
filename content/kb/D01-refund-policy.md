# D01 – Refund Policy

## D01.1 Refund Policy – Period Already Used

Questions this answers: refund milega kya · cancel karu toh paisa wapas · loss hua toh refund

1. All sales are final. No refund is given for the paid period of services already availed by the client.
2. Complaints or dissatisfaction about the quality of services during the paid period do not entitle the client to any refund or compensation.
3. Refunds are not given for the period already availed, irrespective of the client's satisfaction with the recommendations or the outcome of trades.

## D01.2 Refund Policy – Cancellation and Unused Period

Questions this answers: refund milega kya · cancel karu toh paisa wapas · loss hua toh refund

1. As per SEBI guidelines, if a client asks to cancel the subscription, a refund is issued only for the UNUSED portion of the subscription period.
2. The refund is calculated on a pro-rata basis, after deducting charges for services already availed, including applicable taxes and administrative fees.

2. Profits and losses from using TG Levels recommendations are solely the responsibility of the client.

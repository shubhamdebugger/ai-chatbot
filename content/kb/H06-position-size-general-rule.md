# H06 – Position Size – The General Rule in Trading Books

## H06.1 Position Size – The General Rule

Questions this answers: position size kaise nikale · kitni quantity le · position sizing kya hai

1. Standard trading books describe a general rule for position size. It is explained here as education only.
2. Step 1: decide the maximum loss you are willing to accept on one trade.
3. Step 2: find the distance between the entry price and the stop-loss price.
4. Position size = maximum acceptable loss ÷ distance between entry and stop-loss.
5. In options the result is rounded down to whole lots, because options trade only in lots set by the exchange.

## H06.2 Position Size – What the Rule Shows

Questions this answers: stop loss door ho toh quantity kam kyun · wide stop loss matlab kya

1. For the same maximum loss, a wider stop-loss means a smaller position and a tighter stop-loss allows a larger one.
2. The rule limits the planned loss only. In a fast market or a gap, the actual loss can be larger than planned.

## H06.3 Position Size – Money Never to Trade With

Questions this answers: loan leke trade karu · emergency fund se trade kar sakte hai · udhaar ke paise se trading

1. Never trade with money needed for essentials such as household expenses, rent, school fees or medical needs.
2. Never trade with borrowed money.

## H06.4 Position Size – Education, Not Advice

Questions this answers: aap batao kitna lagau · mere liye kitni quantity · mujhe kitne lot lene chahiye

1. This rule is general education from standard trading books. It is not advice to any person on how much to trade.
2. TG Levels research is the same for all clients and does not set a quantity, lot or capital amount for any client.
3. How much to trade is each client's own decision, based on his own situation and risk.
4. Following a sizing rule does not guarantee profit; trading in the securities market carries market risk.

# H02 – Standard Do's and Don'ts for Trading

## H02.1 Standard Do's and Don'ts – Purpose

Questions this answers: trading ke do's and don'ts

1. Before a client starts trading by referring to TG Levels research alerts / reports, TG Levels informs the client of best trading practices for better trading results.
2. The stock market is uncertain and volatile in nature; the points below are general practice to follow.

## H02.2 Standard Do's and Don'ts – Stop Loss and Target

Questions this answers: stop loss kahan lagaye · target order

1. Always trade / invest using a stop loss.
2. Always place the stop loss in the trading terminal; do not keep a mental stop loss.
3. Place a target order in the system at the price at which you want to book profit.

## H02.3 Standard Do's and Don'ts – Capital Protection

Questions this answers: kitna paisa lagaye · ek trade mein kitna

1. Always trade with only a portion of your capital.
2. Never trade with your entire capital in a single trade.
3. Avoid trading with leverage as much as possible.
4. Always focus on protecting capital; with systematic trading, profit can be made in the long term if capital is protected.

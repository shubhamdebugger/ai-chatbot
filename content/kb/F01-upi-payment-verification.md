# F01 – SEBI-Validated UPI – How to Verify Before Paying

## F01.1 UPI – SEBI Requirement

Questions this answers: payment kaise kare · UPI ID sahi hai kaise check kare · payment fail ho gaya

1. SEBI provides a validated UPI mechanism for registered intermediaries such as Research Analysts.
2. TG Levels' official payment bank is HDFC.
3. Actual payment details (UPI ID, QR code, account, payment link) are shared only through TG Levels' official payment system / official chat.

## F01.2 UPI – Payment Modes Allowed

Questions this answers: payment kaise kare · UPI ID sahi hai kaise check kare · payment fail ho gaya

1. Investors may pay by UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS or cheque.
2. If paying by UPI to a registered intermediary, pay ONLY to the new validated UPI ID allotted to that intermediary.

## F01.3 UPI – What to Check Before Paying

Questions this answers: payment kaise kare · UPI ID sahi hai kaise check kare · payment fail ho gaya

1. The left side of "@" shows the intermediary name and category abbreviation (for a Research Analyst: ".ra").
2. The right side of "@" is the exclusive "@valid" handle followed by the bank name.
3. The payment confirmation screen shows a white thumbs-up icon inside a green triangle.
4. The QR code shows the same validation icon and the same UPI ID.

## F01.4 UPI – Do Investors Need a New UPI Handle?

Questions this answers: payment kaise kare · UPI ID sahi hai kaise check kare · payment fail ho gaya

1. No. The new UPI IDs are for intermediaries only. Investors continue to use their existing UPI IDs.

## F01.5 UPI – If Payment Fails

Questions this answers: payment kaise kare · UPI ID sahi hai kaise check kare · payment fail ho gaya

1. The validated UPI ID uses the same banking channel as earlier UPI handles.
2. For technical difficulty, contact your own bank.

## F01.6 UPI – Verify on SEBI Portal

Questions this answers: payment kaise kare · UPI ID sahi hai kaise check kare · payment fail ho gaya

1. SEBI UPI Handle Verification Facility: https://siportal.sebi.gov.in/intermediary/sebi-check
2. It confirms whether a UPI ID is SEBI-registered and officially validated, and shows the intermediary name and category.

## F01.8 Payment – Methods Accepted by TG Levels

Questions this answers: payment kaise kare · UPI ID sahi hai kaise check kare · payment fail ho gaya

1. UPI ID.
2. QR code.
3. Bank transfer.
4. Payment link for the selected plan.
5. The actual payment details are sent only in TG Levels' official chat — never read out on a call and never sent from a personal number.

# H04 – Overtrading and Revenge Trading

## H04.1 Overtrading and Revenge Trading – What They Are

Questions this answers: overtrading kya hai · revenge trading kya hai · main bahut zyada trade karta hoon

1. Overtrading means taking more trades than one's own plan allows, often out of boredom, excitement or the urge to stay in the market.
2. Revenge trading means trading again quickly after a loss to win the money back.
3. Both usually raise risk, because the trades are taken without the Logic, Risk and Exit checks.

## H04.2 Overtrading and Revenge Trading – Warning Signs

Questions this answers: kaise pata chale main overtrading kar raha hoon · loss recover karne ke liye aur trade karna

1. Taking a new trade soon after a loss to make it back.
2. Increasing the quantity after a loss.
3. Trading without a stop-loss, or moving the stop-loss further away.
4. Feeling restless when not in a trade.
5. Not being able to say why a trade was taken.

## H04.3 Overtrading and Revenge Trading – Daily Limits

Questions this answers: daily limit kaise rakhe · ek din mein kitne trade kare · daily loss limit kya hai

1. A common discipline rule is to fix, before the market opens, a maximum number of trades and a maximum loss for the day.
2. When either limit is reached, trading stops for that day.
3. Each trader chooses these limits for himself; TG Levels does not set them for any client.
4. Daily limits support discipline; they do not remove the risk of loss.

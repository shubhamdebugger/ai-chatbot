# B02 – Subscription Plans, Prices and Delivery Apps

## B02.1 Research Plans – Prices and Inclusions

Questions this answers: kitna hai · fees kya hai · plan kya hai · 3 month ka kitna · 1 saal ka kitna

1. Paid research is TG Levels' main service. Prices are total fees inclusive of GST, not monthly amounts.

| Plan | Research duration | Total fee (GST inclusive) | Research | Recorded courses (MMTC and MMOC) | Stock equity swing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner's Plan | 1 month | ₹5,999 | Nifty Options | Not included | Not included |
| Economy Plan | 3 months | ₹17,999 | Nifty Options | Included at no additional charge | Only when a suitable opportunity exists |
| Saver Plan | 6 months | ₹27,999 | Nifty Options | Included at no additional charge | Only when a suitable opportunity exists |
| Super Saver Plan | 12 months | ₹36,999 | Nifty Options | Included at no additional charge | Only when a suitable opportunity exists |

## B02.2 Research Scope – What Is Active and What Is Paused

Questions this answers: options ke calls milenge · commodity / natgas calls milte hai kya · stock options calls · swing calls kab aate hain

1. Active research: Nifty Options.
2. Equity (stock) Options and Commodities research are paused. No resume date is confirmed.
3. Stock equity swing research is given only when the overall market is judged to be in an uptrend and a suitable opportunity is identified. There is no fixed or daily number of swing calls.
4. Swing research is part of the 3-month, 6-month and 12-month plans on that condition. It is not part of the 1-month plan.

## B02.3 Recorded Courses – Access

Questions this answers: course free hai kya · 1 month mein course milega · course kitne din chalega

1. The recorded MMTC and MMOC courses are included at no additional charge in the 3-month, 6-month and 12-month plans.
2. The 1-month plan does not include courses.
3. Recorded course access lasts 1 year in the MMTC Learning App.

## B02.4 Delivery Apps – Where Research and Courses Arrive

Questions this answers: levels kaise aayenge · calls kahan milte hain · app konsa hai · course kahan dekhein

1. Research alerts are delivered in the TG Lite app.
2. Telegram is used as a supplementary research-notification channel.
3. The recorded courses (Advanced Technical Analysis — MMTC, and Advanced Options — MMOC) are in the MMTC Learning App.
4. These are separate apps with separate purposes.

# H05 – What to Do After a Loss

## H05.1 After a Loss – First Steps

Questions this answers: bahut loss ho gaya · loss ke baad kya karein · sab doob gaya · loss hua ab kya karu

1. Losing trades are part of trading; every trader has them.
2. Pause. Avoid taking a new trade the same day to win the money back.
3. If the daily loss limit has been reached, stop trading for that day.
4. Do not add fresh money or borrow money to make a loss back.

## H05.2 After a Loss – Calm Review the Next Day

Questions this answers: loss se kaise seekhe · trade review kaise kare · galti kaise pata kare

1. Review the trade the next day, when calm: was the Logic clear, was the stop-loss placed, was the exit followed?
2. Write down what happened and one thing to do differently next time.
3. Consider trading a smaller size until discipline and confidence return.
4. No method, plan or course can promise to recover a loss; market risk always remains.

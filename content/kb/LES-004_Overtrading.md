Question forms:
- Main bahut zyada trade karta hoon
- overtrading kya hai
- loss recover karne ke liye aur trade
- revenge trading

Answer:
Overtrading means taking trades without a plan — often to recover a loss quickly.
Signs: many trades in a day, bigger size after a loss, trading from boredom or anger.
Fix: decide before the market opens how many trades and how much total loss you allow for the day; stop when either is reached.
Trading to 'recover' a loss usually adds to it.

Never say:
- Any promise that a method recovers losses.

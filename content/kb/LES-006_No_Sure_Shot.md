Question forms:
- 100% sure shot call chahiye
- accuracy kitni hai
- guaranteed profit
- pakka profit wala call
- sure shot

Answer:
No registered research provider can promise profit or accuracy — markets are uncertain, and SEBI does not allow such claims.
Anyone offering 'sure-shot' or '100%' calls is a warning sign.
What a trader can control is the process: a clear reason, a defined risk and a planned exit for every trade — so wrong trades stay planned and small.

Never say:
- Any accuracy figure or past result.

# B01 – Research Update – Format and Contents

## B01.1 Research Update – Header on Every Page

Questions this answers: research update kaun issue karta hai · report pe kya likha hota hai

1. Issued by: TG Levels Private Limited, SEBI Registered Research Analyst, Registration No. INH000026071.

## B01.2 Research Update – Fields in Each Recommendation

Questions this answers: levels kaise aayenge · call kaisa milta hai · entry target stop loss milega kya · CE PE

1. Instrument (e.g. NIFTY) and side (Buy).
2. Strike price and option type (CE = Call, PE = Put).
3. Entry price.
4. Target price.
5. Stop-loss price.
6. Expiry date.
7. Brief rationale — the reason for the recommendation.

## B01.3 Research Report – Disclosures Printed in Every Report

Questions this answers: report mein disclosure kya hota hai · aapka koi conflict of interest hai kya

1. Every research update carries TG Levels' regulatory disclosures: no regulatory action against the firm, no financial interest of the RA or staff in the securities discussed, no known conflict of interest, and no compensation from the companies discussed.
2. TG Levels offers only research and opinion-based analysis — no portfolio management, advisory, brokerage or investment banking.
3. The official source for research updates and disclosures is https://tglevels.com/.
4. Every research update carries the standard risk warning: investments in the market are subject to market risk.

## B01.4 Research Update – How Many Per Day (Nifty Options)

Questions this answers: din mein kitni calls aati hain · roz kitne levels milte hain · kitne trade milenge

1. TG Levels generally provides 3–4 Nifty Options research updates a day.
2. Typically 2–3 come in the first half of the day and 1–2 in the second half.
3. Depending on market conditions and suitable opportunities, the number may vary — for example 2, or 5–6, or more.
4. These numbers are indicative. There is no guaranteed daily count and no fixed maximum.

## B01.5 Research Update – Follow-up Updates and Daily Summary

Questions this answers: exit kab batate ho · trailing stop loss milta hai kya · din ke end mein summary milti hai kya

1. After a research update, the analyst issues live follow-up updates where applicable, such as a trailing stop-loss, an exit, or a lot adjustment.
2. An end-of-day trade summary is provided for reference in the relevant group.
3. A follow-up update is not a new research call.

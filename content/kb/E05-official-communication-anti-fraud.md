# E05 – Official Communication and Anti-Fraud Notice

## E05.1 Anti-Fraud – Official Email Sender

Questions this answers: ye message aapka hai kya · kisi ne OTP maanga · fake call aaya · offer email aapka hai kya

1. Offers, promotions, discounts and sales emails are sent ONLY from contact@tglevels.com.
2. Be cautious of unauthorised entities imitating TG Levels communication channels.

## E05.2 Anti-Fraud – What TG Levels Will Never Ask

Questions this answers: ye message aapka hai kya · kisi ne OTP maanga · fake call aaya · offer email aapka hai kya

1. TG Levels will never ask for your Demat / Trading Login ID, password, security questions or OTP.
2. No TG Levels employee may accept personal gifts, benefits or payments.
3. Payments are valid ONLY to the official company bank account / SEBI-validated UPI ID. Personal transfers are not recognised.
4. TG Levels does not take funds to invest on your behalf.

## E05.3 Anti-Fraud – What to Do if Something Looks Fake

Questions this answers: ye message aapka hai kya · kisi ne OTP maanga · fake call aaya · offer email aapka hai kya

1. Do not share details and do not pay.
2. Email contact@tglevels.com or complaint@tglevels.com, or call customer support, to confirm.

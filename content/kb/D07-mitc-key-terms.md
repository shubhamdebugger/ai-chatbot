# D07 – Most Important Terms and Conditions (MITC) – Key Terms

## D07.1 MITC – Research Service, Not Trade Execution

Questions this answers: aap mere liye trade karoge kya · account handle karte ho kya

1. The terms and consent are for the research services provided by the Research Analyst (RA).
2. The RA cannot execute any trade (buy or sell) on behalf of the client. Clients should not permit the RA to execute trades for them.

## D07.2 MITC – Fee Limit and Advance Fees

Questions this answers: maximum fees kitni ho sakti hai · advance fees kitni · SEBI fee limit

1. For individual and HUF clients, the fee is subject to the maximum prescribed by SEBI / RAASB. The current limit is ₹1,51,000 per year per family for all research services of the RA.
2. The fee limit does not include statutory charges and does not apply to non-individual clients or accredited investors.
3. Fees may be charged in advance, if agreed by the client, for up to one year.
4. If the service ends early, by the client or the RA, the client may seek a refund of proportionate fees for the unexpired period only.

## D07.3 MITC – Payment Modes

Questions this answers: cash mein pay kar sakte hai kya · payment kaise kare

1. Fees may be paid by cheque, online bank transfer, UPI and similar specified modes.
2. Cash payment is not allowed.
3. Clients may optionally pay through CeFCoM (Centralized Fee Collection Mechanism) managed by BSE Limited.

## D07.4 MITC – No Guaranteed Returns

Questions this answers: profit guarantee hai kya · assured return scheme hai kya

1. Assured, guaranteed or fixed-return schemes are prohibited by law and are not offered.
2. The RA cannot guarantee returns, profits, accuracy or risk-free investments.
3. Investments based on research are subject to market risk; SEBI registration, RAASB enlistment and NISM certification do not guarantee performance or returns.

## D07.5 MITC – Grievance Steps

Questions this answers: complaint kaise kare · complaint ke steps · SEBI mein complaint

1. Step 1: contact TG Levels at complaint@tglevels.com or through https://tglevels.com/escalation-matrix/.
2. Step 2: if not satisfied, lodge the grievance on SEBI SCORES at www.scores.sebi.gov.in.
3. Step 3: the client may use Online Dispute Resolution through the Smart ODR portal at https://smartodr.in.

## D07.6 MITC – Client Responsibilities and Account Safety

Questions this answers: OTP share karna hai kya · contact details update

1. Clients must keep their email ID and mobile number updated with the RA.
2. The RA will never ask for login credentials or OTPs of the client's trading, demat or bank account. Never share them with anyone.

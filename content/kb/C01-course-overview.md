# C01 – Course Overview – MMTC and MMOC

## C01.1 Course Overview – The Two Courses

Questions this answers: course kaunse hai · MMTC MMOC kya hai

1. MMTC — Advanced Technical Analysis (technical analysis / price action).
2. MMOC — Advanced Options (options).
3. TG Levels provides both courses.

## C01.2 Course Overview – What Is Included

Questions this answers: course mein kya milega · live class hai kya · scanner milega kya

1. MMTC pre-recorded videos in the MMTC Learning App — about 10 hours (Levels 0 to 4).
2. MMOC pre-recorded videos in the MMTC Learning App — about 16 hours (Day 0 to Day 6).
3. MMTC live sessions on Zoom — 12 sessions × 2 hours = 24 hours.
4. MMOC live sessions on Zoom — 17 sessions × 2 hours = 34 hours.
5. Friday Booster Sessions after the live sessions are complete.
6. Swing, Intraday and Scalping scanners.

## C01.3 Course Overview – Totals

Questions this answers: kitne ghante ka course hai · recorded kitna hai

1. Recorded: about 26 hours (10 + 16).
2. Live: 58 hours (24 + 34).
3. Platforms: MMTC Learning App (iOS / Android) and Zoom.

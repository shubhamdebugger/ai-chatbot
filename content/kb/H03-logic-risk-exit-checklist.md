# H03 – Logic, Risk and Exit – Checklist Before Any Trade

## H03.1 Logic, Risk and Exit – Three Questions Before Any Trade

Questions this answers: trade lene se pehle kya dekhna chahiye · kisi bhi call ko kaise check karein · trade checklist kya hai

1. Before any trade, a trader can ask three questions: Logic, Risk and Exit.
2. Logic: why this trade? Is the reason for entering clear?
3. Risk: how much can I lose? Where is the stop-loss, and is that loss acceptable to me?
4. Exit: when will I exit? At the target, at the stop-loss, or at a time decided in advance.
5. If any one of the three answers is not clear, many traders choose to skip that trade.
6. The checklist builds discipline. It does not guarantee a profit on any trade.

## H03.2 Logic, Risk and Exit – Using the Checklist on Any Call

Questions this answers: kisi call ko checklist se kaise check kare · call lene se pehle risk kaise samjhe

1. The Risk and Exit answers come from the stop-loss and the target given with a trade idea; if either is missing, the risk is unknown.
2. Whether to act on an update, and with how much, remains the client's own decision.

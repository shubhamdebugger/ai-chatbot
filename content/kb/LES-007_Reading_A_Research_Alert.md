Question forms:
- alert kaise padhein
- entry above ka matlab
- SL aur target kya hai alert mein
- research alert samajh nahi aata

Answer:
A research alert has four parts:
1. Reason — why the research team sees this setup.
2. Entry — the price condition at which the idea becomes valid (for example 'above' a level).
3. Stop-loss — where the idea is wrong and the trade is exited.
4. Target(s) — where profit may be booked in steps.
Read it with Logic · Risk · Exit. Whether to act on any alert is your own decision; Tanya explains the format only and never comments on a specific alert.

Never say:
- Whether to take or skip a specific alert.

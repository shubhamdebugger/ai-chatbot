Question forms:
- Kitna paisa lagana chahiye?
- How much capital per trade?
- position size kaise nikalte hain
- lot kitne lene chahiye

Answer:
Position size answers: how much can I lose on this one trade?
Step 1: decide the maximum loss you accept on a single trade — many traders keep it a small part of their trading capital.
Step 2: find the distance between entry and stop-loss.
Step 3: size = maximum loss ÷ distance. A wider stop-loss means a smaller position.
This keeps any one wrong trade small enough to recover from. Never trade with money needed for essentials, or borrowed money.

Never say:
- A specific amount he should invest; any income promise.

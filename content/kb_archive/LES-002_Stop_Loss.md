Question forms:
- Stop-loss kya hota hai?
- What is a stop loss?
- SL kyun lagate hain?
- stop loss hit ho gaya

Answer:
A stop-loss is the price, decided before entry, at which you exit if the trade goes wrong.
Why it matters: it turns an unknown loss into a planned one, so one bad trade cannot wipe out weeks of work.
Limits: in a fast move or a gap, the exit can happen beyond the stop-loss price. A stop-loss limits losses; it does not prevent them.
A stop-loss that is hit often usually means entries are late or the stop is placed too close — worth reviewing, not removing.

Never say:
- A stop-loss level for any specific trade.

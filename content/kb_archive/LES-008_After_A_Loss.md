Question forms:
- bahut loss ho gaya
- loss ke baad kya karein
- I lost money today
- sab doob gaya

Answer:
After a big loss: pause first — no new trade the same day to 'win it back'.
Next day, review calmly: was there a reason, a defined risk and a planned exit? Which one was missing?
Reduce size until confidence returns. Losses are part of trading; the goal is to keep each one planned and small.

Never say:
- Any promise of loss recovery.

Question forms:
- SEBI registered ho kya
- fraud to nahi ho
- registration kaise check karein
- are you genuine

Answer:
Anyone can verify a Research Analyst on the SEBI website (sebi.gov.in) in the list of registered intermediaries, by name or registration number. TG Level's registration number: see the SEBI registration line (FX-18). Registration does not guarantee performance or returns.

Never say:
- That registration guarantees anything.

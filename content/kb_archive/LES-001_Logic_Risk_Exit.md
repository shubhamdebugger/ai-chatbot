Question forms:
- Trade lene se pehle kya dekhna chahiye?
- How do I judge a call?
- Kisi bhi call ko kaise check karein?
- what to check before a trade

Answer:
Before any trade, ask three questions:
1. Logic — why this trade? What is the reason?
2. Risk — how much can I lose if I am wrong?
3. Exit — when do I get out, in profit and in loss?
If any one of the three is missing, it is a tip, not a plan. This checklist works for any call from any source. Every TG Level research alert carries all three — reason, stop-loss and exit.

Never say:
- Any promise of profit; any view on a specific trade.
